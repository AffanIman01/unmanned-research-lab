# Magnetic recovery — preliminary demand

Not a safe working load, fabrication release or validated control system.

Moving recovery allowance = 8 kg ROV + 2 kg accessories + 1.8 kg yoke + 3.5 kg head + 1 kg retained water + 0.5 kg tether = **16.8 kg**.

Equivalent lift demand = 16.8 × 9.81 × 1.5 + 25 = **272.2 N**.
Resistance screening target = 2 × demand = **544.4 N**.
At assumed 0.06 m mount offset: service moment **16.3 Nm**, screening target **32.7 Nm**.

All factors, masses and offsets except nominal ROV mass are provisional. Target resistance is not an OEM rating. No magnetic structural credit. Wave/slam/fatigue, pin/lug failure, fasteners, actuator geometry, trim and load paths need physical engineering validation.

Current visual lower stop = 65% × 418 mm = **271.7 mm**. Actual stroke and immersion remain unmeasured. This travel is also used in the application's illustrative time/energy calculation. At 0.04 m/s: 6.79 s lift time. Dynamic friction and electrical startup are not modeled.

Computer/enclosure allowance remains 2 kg even though the grey enclosure is omitted from the scene. Weigh the planned installation before updating payload accounting.
