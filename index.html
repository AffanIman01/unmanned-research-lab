Flowing-water recovery

Flow and control notes ↓

Hold the USV into current where propulsion and tether permit. ROV waits below the mount, tracks its relative position and motion, corrects lateral error/yaw, then inserts slowly after a stable alignment window.

Use a docking camera/marker with sonar backup when visibility is poor; DVL/depth/IMU support motion estimation. Actual sensors and CHASING command access remain unresolved.

Stop insertion on stale pose, excessive motion, poor thrust reserve or tether risk. Retreat only before contact through a verified clear corridor. After contact, preserve existing locks and hold.

Demo sliders use a reduced kinematic model. Its gains, tolerances and current limit are assumptions, not measured M2 PRO MAX capabilities. Speed margin is command headroom. HOLD pauses the illustration; it does not model physical station keeping.

Power & tether

Design notes ↓

Example: 24 V × 3 A × 0.2 s × 2 pulses = 0.008 Wh/cycle. Coil only; assumptions, not a selected module.

Reel → dancer/load cell → captive stern fairlead → ROV strain relief. Take up slack only; never pull the ROV into the head.

Stock E-Reel: manual payout with cable outside straightener; powered take-up after reinserting it. Automatic bidirectional handling needs an approved upgrade.

On return: contact → magnet ON → both pins verified → check mechanical load transfer → magnet OFF → recheck pins/seat → lift. EPM OFF requires a pulse; cutting its electricity does not remove attraction.

Mounts and clearances unverified.

Model information

Magnetic: electro-permanent magnet (EPM) uses brief ON/OFF pulses; mechanical pins carry the load. After both locks and load transfer are verified, command an OFF pulse and recheck retention before lifting. EPMs already require no coil holding current; OFF removes magnetic attraction rather than saving EPM hold energy. This is a proposed sealed module, not a selected underwater-rated product. Wet pole-gap performance, corrosion, release residuals, magnetic interference, yoke buoyancy and all structural paths need testing.

Tether: the proposed centerline stern fairlead is placed aft of the reference propeller region. Full sweep, slack, bend radius and propeller clearance are unverified. The OEM E-Reel manual requires manual straightener rerouting between payout and take-up.

EPM principle · industrial reference only · Official E-Reel manual

Restored: the original detailed vehicle models from your ME120 Design Lab. Units are metres. Hull length/beam and the ROV body envelope follow nominal dimensions; individual parts and mounting interfaces remain photo-derived.

Your mount: The magnetic assembly uses the original underside ring and twin moving tubes. The new adapter stays aligned with the ring through the 65% lower stop (271.7 mm modeled travel). No new independent lift tower. The raised cradle remains wet; full-travel clearance, tube engagement and transport stow require measurement.

Published: ME120 current brochure: 2.5 × 1.4 m, 160 kg excluding lifter, 60 kg payload. Older brochures list 45 kg payload. Confirm the vessel revision and loading definition. Neither figure is a lifter rating.

Published: M2 PRO MAX: approximately 8 kg, 608 × 294 × 196 mm excluding floodlights. E-Reel: 3.5 kg; included tether mass is unclear. Weigh the ready-to-operate equipment.

Unmeasured: Ø170/Ø74 mm reference ring, blank adapter and 271.7 mm limited illustrated travel. No mounting-hole pattern is supplied. Support contacts and added masses remain estimates.

Camera views: synthetic renderings from the existing underside PTZ and ROV front lens. USV pan and −80° to 0° tilt are preview settings. FOV and zoom are illustrative. No video stream, real camera or robot control is connected.

Required evidence: lifter/structural load and moment ratings, support contacts, brake and transport lock capacities, motor/actuator geometry across travel, corrosion and fatigue assessment, loaded trim/freeboard and physical collision checks.
