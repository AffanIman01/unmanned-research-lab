Accessories & recovery 03
  • Grabber Arm 2 — animated open / close.
  • Laser Scaler — reference beams and target range.
  • Water Sampler — collection progress with depth / position.
  • Ping360 Sonar — rotating sweep with illustrative echoes.

In 3D Studio, select M2 PRO MAX → Accessories (K). Four separate stations animate each function. In Simulation, press K, select a tool, arm, then test underwater. Disarm stops tool actions.

Recovery concept: align → contact → magnet ON → locks verified → load-transfer check → magnet OFF → retention check → lift.

Hold L for 1.5 seconds in Water Operation to deploy or retrieve; release early to cancel. Accessory fit, sonar echoes and recovery timing are illustrative.

Trials & validation 04
  1. Start in calm water and check each axis.
  2. Enable waves; compare motion and recovery.
  3. In ROV pilot, record the trial and download its log.

Leaving Simulation starts a fresh trial when reopened. Download your ROV log first.

Browser training only. No live vehicle connection. Geometry, water response and hardware reliability still require measurement and field validation.
02 · RESEARCH

Full Report

Working research report and engineering appendices.

Project overview

Integrated USV–ROV deployment and teleoperation research. The ME120 carries the M2 PRO MAX, with a proposed magnetic capture and mechanical retention system. This workspace supports design review and simulation before hardware trials.

System architecture

Proposed communications path: remote operator → Internet / Starlink → onboard gateway → supported vehicle interfaces. The underwater ROV tether remains. RC3 and E-Reel interfaces require separate integration verification.

The browser simulator is a standalone demonstrator; it does not command real equipment.

Recovery design

Approach → align → contact → magnetic capture → verify mechanical locks → verify load transfer → release magnetic attraction → check retention → lift.

Geometry, mounting interfaces, lifter capacity and tether clearance require physical validation. The recovery sequence is illustrative.

Validation & current limits

Verify control axes, stopping behaviour, camera views and repeatable simulation scenarios first. Then measure the mounting interfaces, obtain load ratings and conduct supervised capture and recovery trials.

Browser results are not calibrated ME120 or M2 PRO MAX performance. This working report does not claim completed field validation.

03 · SURFACE PLATFORM

USV Documentation

OceanAlpha ME120 · hardware and integration.

Platform & mounted equipment

OceanAlpha ME120 multi-payload USV. Project equipment includes front and rear Harxon HX-CSX627A GNSS antennas, Hikvision PTZ, ZED stereo camera, RoboSense 3D LiDAR, two mmWave radars and navigation lighting.

Jetson Orin NX integration is planned. Component positions in the studio are reference geometry.

Radio & ground station

Antenna order supplied for this project: 1.4 GHz, 4G, 2.4 GHz, 1.4 GHz. Antennas 1 and 4 match the ground-control link.

Starlink Mini provides the proposed Internet backhaul. Ground equipment includes a laptop, controller and two tall antenna stands.

Lift & integration records

Confirm your vessel revision, payload definition, lifter working load, stroke and mounting dimensions before hardware integration. A vessel payload rating is not a lifter rating.

04 · UNDERWATER PLATFORM

ROV Documentation

CHASING M2 PRO MAX · tether, tools and recovery.

Platform & tether connection

CHASING M2 PRO MAX. The project reference chain is ROV → tether → E-Reel → RC3 controller. Internet teleoperation requires a separately verified gateway and control interface.

Accessories

Four demonstration accessories: Grabber Arm 2, Laser Scaler, Water Sampler and Ping360 Sonar. Open the accessory view in 3D Studio to explore their animations.

Animated operation does not establish physical compatibility or validated sensing performance.

Handling & recovery

Support the ROV body in the cradle. Do not lift by the tether or battery pull ring. Verify configured dimensions with lights and accessories fitted.
